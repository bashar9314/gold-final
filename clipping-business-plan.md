# The Clipping Business: How I'd Get to $4,000+/Month
*Written September 2026. Sources at the bottom.*

---

## 1. What clipping is (in one minute)

Creators (podcasters, streamers, coaches, brands) have hours of long video. They need it on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts as **short 15–60 second clips**, because that's where new fans come from. A **clipper** cuts the best moments, adds captions and a hook, and posts or delivers them.

There are **two ways clippers get paid**:

| | **A. Campaigns (pay per view)** | **B. Clients (monthly retainer)** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Whop Content Rewards, Vyro, Clipping Discords | You sell directly to creators / businesses |
| Pay | ~$0.50–$3 per 1,000 views (avg ~$1) | $500–$1,500+ per client per month |
| Needs | No followers, no experience | A small portfolio + **sales skill** |
| Income | Unpredictable. Beginners: $100–$500/mo. Grinders: $1–3K/mo | Predictable. 4 clients = $4K+/mo |
| Risk | A clip can flop and pay $0 | Client can cancel |

**The honest truth:** Most people who make $4K a month from clipping **don't** get there from campaign views alone. Only the top grinders do that. The steady money comes from **retainer clients** or from **running a small team**. That's exactly where your sales skill makes you better positioned than 95% of clippers, who are mostly teenagers who can edit but can't sell.

## 2. If I were you, here's the play

> **Learn the craft on campaigns (weeks 1–2) → sell retainers to clients (weeks 2–6) → hire clippers and become the agency (month 2+).**

Campaigns give you practice, a portfolio, and some cash. Retainers get you to $4K. The agency takes you past it.

### Your unfair edge: combine it with cars
You know the car business. **Car dealers, car salespeople, detailers, wrap shops, auto YouTubers and car podcasts** all have video and nobody clipping it. You can walk into a dealership and talk to the GM in their language. A 19-year-old clipper can't. That's a niche you can own.

## 3. Your 30-day plan

### Week 1: Set up and learn by doing (cost: $0)
- [ ] **Free tools:** CapCut (editing + auto captions), plus a free tier of an AI clip-finder (OpusClip, Vizard, or Ssemble) to speed up finding moments.
- [ ] **Create fresh accounts** on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts dedicated to clipping. Spend 2–3 days using them normally (scroll, like, follow in the niche) before posting. New accounts that post immediately often get suppressed.
- [ ] **Sign up on Whop and browse "Content Rewards."** Pick 2–3 campaigns in niches you understand (business, cars, motivation, podcasts). Read each campaign's rules carefully (required hashtags, tagging, disclosure, which platforms count).
- [ ] **Post 3–5 clips per day.** Every clip needs:
  1. **A hook in the first 1–2 seconds** (text on screen + the strongest line first).
  2. **Big, bold captions** (most people watch with sound off).
  3. **Vertical 9:16**, tight cuts, no dead air.
  4. **One idea per clip.** The clip ends right after the payoff.

### Week 2: Find what works + build the portfolio
- [ ] Look at your stats. Which hooks held people? Do more of those.
- [ ] Save your **3 best clips** plus their view counts. That's your portfolio.
- [ ] Target by day 14: **first campaign payout ($50–$300)** and proof you can get views.

### Weeks 2–4: Sell retainers (this is where the $4K comes from)
**Who to sell to:**
1. **Local businesses already filming**: car dealers, real estate agents, gyms, med spas, lawyers, restaurants. Many post long videos or lives on Facebook/YouTube with no short clips.
2. **Podcasters and YouTubers with 5K–100K subscribers**: big enough to pay, too small to have a team.
3. **Coaches and course sellers**: every clip is a free ad for them, so they understand the value.

**Your offer (keep it simple):**
| Package | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 12 clips/month, captioned, ready to post | $500/mo |
| Growth | 30 clips/month + you post them to their accounts | $1,000/mo |
| Dealer Special | Walkaround + testimonial + sales tips clips, 20/month | $750–$1,200/mo |

**$4,000/month = 4 Growth clients, or 5–6 Starter/Dealer clients.**

**Close with a free sample:** Take one of their existing videos, make **3 free clips**, and send them. *"I already made these for you. Want 30 of these every month?"* This works because they see the result before paying.

### Month 2+: Become the agency
- [ ] Hire 2–3 clippers (Discord clipping communities, Upwork, or promising teens from your campaigns) at **$5–$15 per clip**.
- [ ] You sell and check quality, and they edit.
- [ ] Math: a $1,000/month client needs 30 clips. At $10 a clip that's $300 in labor, so you keep **$700**. With 10 clients, you **keep about $7,000/month** without editing anything yourself.

## 6. Rules so you don't lose the money
- **Only clip content you have permission to use**: campaign content, or your clients' content. Reposting random creators' videos without permission can get your accounts struck and your earnings withheld.
- **Never buy views or use bots.** Campaigns check, and you'll be banned and unpaid.
- **Disclose paid content.** When you're paid to promote something, the FTC requires a clear disclosure (#ad or the platform's "paid partnership" label). Follow each campaign's disclosure rules.
- **Get a simple written agreement for every retainer** (scope, number of clips, price, payment due on the 1st, 30-day cancel). Get paid upfront each month.
- **Taxes:** this is self-employment income. Set aside 25–30% of every payment.
